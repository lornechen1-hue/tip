📦 Kakobuy Shipping & Costs

A Complete Guide for Users — practical tips to help you understand shipping and save money.
🔍 1. Use the Shipping Estimator

On the Kakobuy homepage, there's a shipping estimator at the top. Just enter your destination country to see an estimated cost and available shipping routes.

⚠️ Important: This is just a reference. The actual shipping cost is usually lower than the estimate.
📐 2. Always Do a “Rehearsal” Before Shipping

This is the most practical money‑saving feature on Kakobuy — but a lot of new users don't know about it.

  • What it is: Before you officially ship your items, you can request a “Rehearsal”. The warehouse will measure the actual volume and weight of your package.
  • Why it matters: Shipping is calculated based on real data, which can significantly reduce your costs.
  • Cost: Only around 20 CNY (~$3 USD) — and you'll save way more than that on shipping.
💡 If you skip the rehearsal, you'll be charged based on estimated volume, which is often higher than the actual cost. Don't overpay!
⚡ 3. Sensitive Items = Higher Shipping Costs

If you're buying items with built‑in batteries (electronics, gadgets, etc.) or liquids (perfume, skincare, etc.), those are considered “sensitive goods.” They require special shipping routes, which are more expensive than standard routes.

Pro tip to save money:

  • ❌ Don't pack sensitive items together with regular items (clothes, shoes, accessories) in one package.
  • ✅ Split them into two separate packages: regular items go via the normal route, sensitive items go via the special route.

This can actually lower your total shipping cost significantly.

⚖️ 4. What's the Best Weight to Buy?
1‑2 kg Not very cost‑effective. The shipping cost doesn't justify the value of the items — unless it's your first order and you're using a new‑user coupon.
5‑12 kg Sweet spot Shipping cost per kg drops significantly, and you'll have access to more shipping coupons with better discounts.
⚠️ Watch out: Shipping is calculated based on the greater of actual weight or volumetric weight. If you buy something bulky but lightweight (like a large plush toy), the shipping cost may be surprisingly high. For items like that, Kakobuy might not be your best option.
✈️ 5. Which Shipping Route Should You Choose?
Route Pros Cons
EMS Cheaper Slower
UPS / DHL Faster More expensive

If you're not in a rush, EMS is more budget‑friendly. If you need it fast, go with UPS / DHL.

🎟️ 6. Coupon Tips
  • Each coupon can only be used once per account.
  • Need to place multiple orders? Simply create a new account — this allows you to reuse the same coupons. In a sense, it's unlimited.

New users get the best coupon deals, so it's very friendly for first‑time orders.
